01 / Introduction

A Village.
A Landscape.
A Story.

Every village tells a story through its landscape, architecture, archaeological remains and the people who have shaped it through generations.

This digital atlas explores the spatial and cultural character of a Lebanese village. Through historical documentation, spatial analysis and field observations, we examine how its landscape has evolved and how its heritage continues to shape its identity.

03 / Architectural Heritage

Architecture and memory

Buildings are physical records of cultural identity, construction traditions and the everyday lives of their inhabitants.

Illustrative photograph · Traditional architectural reference.

Historic houses, religious structures and communal buildings form an architectural network that reflects the village's social and historical development.

Field documentation can record construction materials, building typologies, architectural elements, condition and transformations. These observations can be linked directly to individual mapped structures.

Architecture preserves the memory of a place through its material fabric.

04 / Natural & Agricultural Landscape

The village and its environment

Human settlements are closely connected to their natural surroundings. Terrain, water, vegetation and agricultural practices all contribute to the character of a landscape.

Illustrative photograph · Replace with local landscape imagery.

Olive groves, agricultural terraces and cultivated fields are not simply environmental features. They also represent historical land use and traditional relationships between communities and their surroundings.

Combining elevation models, land-use mapping and field observations allows us to investigate how the natural landscape influenced settlement patterns.

05 / Routes & Connectivity

Paths that connect places

Roads and paths establish relationships between different parts of a settlement. They connect homes, public spaces, religious buildings and agricultural areas.

Illustrative photograph · Rural landscape and pathway.

The spatial analysis of historic routes can reveal how movement, accessibility and topography shaped the development of the village.

Mapping these connections allows us to investigate the relationships between settlement density, public spaces and surrounding landscapes.

06 / Conclusion

Understanding place through space

The village is a complex cultural landscape shaped by history, environment, architecture and human activity. Integrating these dimensions through GIS creates a dynamic framework for documenting, interpreting and communicating heritage.

This atlas is an evolving documentation project. Each mapped feature contributes another element to the wider story of the village.
