Install an app to your home screen
  1. Open the app itself (tap it above) — not this page.
  2. Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. It gets its own icon and opens full-screen, without the browser bar.

Repeat for each one you want. They no longer block each other.

Installed it but can't find the icon? Android puts installed web apps in the app drawer (swipe up from the bottom), not always on the home screen. Find it there, press and hold, then drag it onto your home screen. That's your shortcut — it opens straight into the app with no browser bar, which is the same thing Chrome's Open app button does.
